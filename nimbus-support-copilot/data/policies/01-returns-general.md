# General Returns Policy

Nimbus Electronics accepts returns on most products within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is in original condition with all accessories, cables, and packaging included.

To start a return:
1. Log in to your Nimbus account and open the order under "My Orders."
2. Select "Return this item" and choose a reason.
3. Print the prepaid return label and drop the package at any authorized courier point within 7 days.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of the warehouse receiving and inspecting the item. Items returned without original packaging may be subject to a 15% restocking fee.

This general policy does not apply to opened electronics, final-sale items, or trade-in devices — see the specific policies for those categories.
