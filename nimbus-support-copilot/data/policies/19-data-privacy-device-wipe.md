# Data Privacy and Device Wipe Policy

Before returning, trading in, or sending in any device for repair, customers are responsible for backing up and factory-resetting it to remove personal data. Nimbus is not liable for personal data left on a returned or traded-in device, though as a courtesy, Nimbus's warehouse wipes all received devices before resale or recycling.

Support agents should always remind customers to back up and wipe a device before it ships back, regardless of return reason — this should be mentioned proactively, not only when asked.
