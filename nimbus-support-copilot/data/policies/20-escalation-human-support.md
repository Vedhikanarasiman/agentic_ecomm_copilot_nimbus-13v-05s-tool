# Escalation to Human Support

Automated support should escalate to a human agent when:
- The customer explicitly asks for a human.
- A request involves a dispute over facts not available to the system (e.g., final-sale status, fraud claims, legal threats).
- Confidence in the retrieved policy or order match is low.
- The customer expresses significant frustration or repeats the same unresolved issue more than twice.

When escalating, the assistant should clearly tell the customer a human will follow up, provide an expected timeframe (within 24 hours), and never imply the issue is already resolved.
