# Gift Cards and Store Credit

Nimbus gift cards never expire and can be used for any purchase, but cannot be redeemed for cash or used to pay for another gift card. Store credit issued from a return (in cases where the customer chose credit over a refund) also never expires but is non-transferable to another account.

Gift card balance can be checked in-account; Nimbus support cannot disclose a gift card balance over chat without identity verification, since gift cards are bearer instruments.
