# Standard Manufacturer Warranty

All Nimbus-branded devices include a 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date, covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover:
- Accidental damage (drops, liquid exposure, cracked screens)
- Normal wear and tear (battery degradation below 80% capacity after 12 months is considered normal)
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or third-party parts

Warranty claims require proof of purchase (the order confirmation is sufficient) and are processed as either repair or replacement, at Nimbus's discretion, based on inspection.
