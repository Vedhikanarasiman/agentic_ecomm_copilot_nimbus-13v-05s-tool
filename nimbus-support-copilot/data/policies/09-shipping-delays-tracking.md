# Shipping Delays and Tracking Issues

Tracking numbers update within 24 hours of the courier picking up the package. If tracking shows no movement for more than 5 business days (domestic) or 10 business days (international), this is treated as a delayed shipment, not a lost one.

A shipment is only classified "lost" if there is no tracking movement for 10 business days (domestic) or 21 days (international) — before that threshold, do not offer a refund or replacement for "lost" shipment; direct the customer to wait or escalate to carrier investigation.
