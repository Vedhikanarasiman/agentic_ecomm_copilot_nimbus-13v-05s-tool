# Returns Policy — Opened Electronics

Laptops, smartphones, cameras, and gaming consoles that have been powered on and used can still be returned within 15 days of delivery (shorter than the standard 30-day window), provided:
- The device has been factory reset and all personal data wiped.
- No physical damage beyond normal inspection wear.
- All original accessories are included.

A 10% opened-item fee applies unless the return reason is a manufacturer defect, in which case no fee applies and the item should instead be processed under the Defective Item Replacement policy, not this one.

Audio accessories (earbuds, in-ear headphones) cannot be returned once the seal is broken, for hygiene reasons, regardless of condition.
