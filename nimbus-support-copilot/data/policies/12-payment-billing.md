# Payment Methods and Billing Issues

Nimbus accepts credit card, debit card, UPI, net banking, and Nimbus Wallet. Failed payments that still show a debit on the customer's bank statement are typically an authorization hold that releases automatically within 5-7 business days — this is not a Nimbus-side charge and Nimbus cannot expedite bank-side hold releases.

Duplicate charges should be verified against the order history first (a customer may have two separate valid orders) before being treated as a billing error and escalated.
