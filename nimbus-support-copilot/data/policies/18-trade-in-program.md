# Trade-In Program

Customers can trade in an eligible used device for instant store credit toward a new Nimbus purchase. Trade-in value is quoted based on the customer's self-reported condition and is confirmed (or adjusted) only after Nimbus receives and inspects the device — the online quote is an estimate, not a guarantee.

If the received device's condition is worse than reported, Nimbus will offer a revised (lower) credit amount before finalizing; the customer can decline and have the device shipped back at no cost within 14 days of the revised offer.
