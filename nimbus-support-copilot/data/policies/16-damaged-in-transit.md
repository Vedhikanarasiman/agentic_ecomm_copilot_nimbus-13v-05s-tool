# Damaged-in-Transit Claims

If a package arrives visibly damaged or the product is broken on arrival, this is a shipping damage claim, not a warranty or defect claim — it must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos of both the packaging and the product.

Claims reported after 48 hours are treated as a standard defect/return case instead, which may carry different fees and timelines — do not apply the no-fee damaged-in-transit process to late-reported claims.
