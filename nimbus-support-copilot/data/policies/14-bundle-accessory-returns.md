# Bundle and Accessory Return Rules

Products purchased as part of a bundle (e.g., laptop + sleeve + mouse at a discounted combined price) must be returned as a complete set. Returning only one component of a bundle voids the bundle discount, and the refund is recalculated at each item's individual list price, not the discounted bundle price — this often results in a smaller refund than the customer expects, and should be explained clearly before processing.
