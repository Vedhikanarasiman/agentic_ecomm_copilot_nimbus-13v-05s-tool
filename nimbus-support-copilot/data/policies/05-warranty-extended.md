# Extended Warranty Plans (Nimbus Care+)

Nimbus Care+ can be purchased at checkout or within 30 days of delivery, extending coverage to 2 or 3 years and adding one accidental damage claim per year (subject to a deductible: $49 for phones/audio, $99 for laptops/monitors/cameras).

Care+ is non-transferable and voided if the device is resold or if damage resulted from gross negligence (e.g., intentional damage, use in conditions the product is not rated for). Cancellation within 30 days of purchase is fully refundable; after that, refunds are prorated.
