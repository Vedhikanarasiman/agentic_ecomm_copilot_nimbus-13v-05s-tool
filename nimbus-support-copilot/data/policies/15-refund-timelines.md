# Refund Timelines and Methods

Refunds are issued to the original payment method: 5-7 business days for cards, 2-3 business days for UPI/net banking, instant for Nimbus Wallet. Nimbus cannot issue refunds to a different payment method or to a third party's account, even at the customer's request.

If a refund shows as "processed" in Nimbus systems but the customer hasn't received it after the stated window, direct them to check with their bank/payment provider first — Nimbus's refund obligation is considered met once it appears as "processed" on Nimbus's side.
